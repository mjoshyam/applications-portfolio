How Many Groups? Finding the Right k
Descriptive
After defining the feature space, we evaluated how many segments best represent the gamer population.
Model Diagnostics — Inertia (Elbow at k=5)
Silhouette Score — k=2 to k=9
Cluster Distribution — Cluster Size Distribution (k=5)
Final Model Selection
k = 5 maximises statistical metrics but creates a micro-cluster (<1%).
k = 4 provides balanced and interpretable gamer segments.

Which Players Suffer Most?
Causal
Same game. Same hours. Consistently different outcomes.
The group you belong to changes everything.
Anxiety (GAD-7) — lower is better
Social Phobia (SPIN) — lower is better
Life Satisfaction (SWLS) — higher is better
Escapist Regulators score 32% higher anxiety than Balanced Recreationists — despite often playing the same games for similar hours.
