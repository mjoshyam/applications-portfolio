The industry has been
measuring the wrong thing
for 30 years.
What the industry tracks
✕ Hours played
✕ Session length
✕ League rank
✕ Streams watched
✕ Daily active users
These measure behavior — what players do. Behavior is just a symptom.
What actually determines outcomes
✓ Why they picked up the controller
✓ What need the game is meeting
✓ Whether alternatives exist
✓ Identity tied to outcomes
✓ Motivation type
These measure motivation — and motivation is what the data proves drives wellbeing.
💡
A player gaming 20 hrs/week to unwind is psychologically healthier than one gaming 8 hrs/week to escape anxiety they can't face elsewhere. The platform can't tell them apart — because it's only watching what they do, not what they need.
